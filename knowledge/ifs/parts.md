A “part” is invitational language for an aspect of someone’s present experience. Ask what the person notices and use only names or meanings they confirm. Do not assign a category, history, diagnosis, or hidden cause. Prefer “Could there be a part of you that feels worried?” over “That is your anxious protector.”
